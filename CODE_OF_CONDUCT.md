# Code of Conduct

Project Coffee Bean adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**. The full text is at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/> and applies to every project
space: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any community chat.

## In short

We want everyone to be able to take part without harassment, whatever their age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

Expected:

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and take feedback about the work, not the person.
- Own mistakes and fix them.

Not acceptable:

- Insults, personal or political attacks, trolling.
- Sexualised language or unwelcome advances.
- Harassment in public or private.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the project lead listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md), through
the contact shown on their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove
comments, commits and contributions that break this code, and may ban people temporarily or
permanently, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
